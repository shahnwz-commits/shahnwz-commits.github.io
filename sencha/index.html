Sencha

The field register app of Sencha Tea Estate. The owner and supervisors use it on Android phones to keep the estate's daily books.

What it does

  • Daily attendance and field work by section: spray, plucking, watering, pruning, drainage.
  • Weekly pay sheets, settled every Friday (the pay week runs Saturday to Friday).
  • Purchases, expenses, leaf sales, receipts, stock of fertiliser and chemicals, and bill photos.
  • Books and reports: day book, ledgers, week and month reports, PDF and Excel export.

Why it uses Google sign-in

Sencha works offline on each phone and keeps every phone in step through the estate's own Google Drive. When you sign in with Google, Sencha asks for:

  • Your email address, to check that the account is the estate's account. Any other account is turned away.
  • Its own app data folder in Google Drive (drive.appdata), to store the estate's records, bill photos and backups. This is a private folder that only Sencha can use. Sencha cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

Sign-in happens in your phone's browser. The Google account is not added to the phone.
