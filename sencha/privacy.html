Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026

This policy explains what the Sencha Estate Android app ("Sencha") does with information. Sencha is the private field register of Sencha Tea Estate and is used only by the estate's owner and supervisors.

Information Sencha keeps

  • Estate records you enter: worker names, attendance, field work, wages and advances, purchases, expenses, sales, receipts, stock, and photos of paper bills.
  • Google account email: read once at sign-in to confirm it is the estate's account.
  • A sign-in token: kept on the phone, encrypted with the Android Keystore, so Sencha can sync without asking you to sign in every time.
  • A random device ID: tells the estate's phones apart when they sync.

Sencha does not collect location, contacts, advertising IDs or analytics, and it shows no ads.

Google user data

Sencha requests these Google permissions:

  • openid and email: to identify the signed-in account and turn away any account that is not the estate's.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata: to read and write files in Sencha's own hidden app data folder in that Google Drive. It holds the estate's records, bill photos, backups and error logs. Sencha cannot see, open or change any other file in the Drive.

Google user data is used only to sign in and to sync and back up the estate's own records between the estate's phones. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone else, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models. No person reads it except the estate's own users inside the app.

Sencha's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored

On each phone, in the app's private storage, and in the app data folder of the estate's Google Drive. Sencha has no server of its own; no data is sent anywhere else.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Records are kept as the estate's books, with a history of changes for audit. Deleted entries leave the books but stay in that history.
  • Daily backups are kept for 14 days, weekly for 8 weeks and monthly for 24 months.
  • To remove Sencha's access: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Sencha.
  • To delete everything Sencha stored in Drive: in Google Drive, open Settings, then Manage apps, then Sencha, and choose "Delete hidden app data".
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the data on that phone.

Security

Sign-in uses Google's browser sign-in with PKCE. The app is locked with a PIN. The sign-in token is encrypted on the phone, and all traffic to Google uses HTTPS.
